## Limits

The automated checks exercise each range/number endpoint, select option, checkbox/radio state and reachable non-reset buttons individually. They do not enumerate all combinations, check every animation frame, simulate an expert reader, validate every physical model or establish WCAG conformance. Tiny-text, overlap and clipping detection are heuristics, not visual certification. The page screenshots show the viewport top; full-page text and every embedded widget location were not all read visually. No claim is made that every PNG was individually inspected or that all 46 widgets received exhaustive expert qualitative approval.

Completed widget cases have no remaining recorded layout/runtime heuristic failures; peer review additionally identified wrapper accumulation, which is addressed and checked by the focused diagram regression. Browser protocol timeouts remain documented as harness concurrency limitations, with successful serial reruns. Proprietary recipe, price and model uncertainty belongs in the course review ledgers, including the independent refutation report, rather than being converted into a visual-QA pass claim.
