# Supplier and jurisdiction framing: visual audit

Reviewed 2026-09-14. Scope: the active widget sources, figure lesson JSON, and app/index interface text. Searches covered supplier, China/Chinese, Taiwan, Western/allied and named Chinese manufacturers; matching figure specifications and widgets were read, with additional review of the node comparison and GPU rack explorer. This is a framing and supplier-coverage audit, not a fresh validation of every numerical or historical claim in the course.

## Corrections

- `geo-share`: removed all inherited country-share proportions. Their source comment named broad analyst/report families without establishing exact dates or consistent denominators. The old End demand row mixed electronics assembly with final buyers, while the equipment row mixed wafer processing, test and packaging. The existing widget ID now renders a qualitative supplier roster with a working country/region filter. No bar length, number, card area or ordering claims a market share. Headquarters are distinguished from factory locations; Siemens EDA is explicitly labeled by its German parent group. Absence from these selected examples does not imply absence of capability.
- The supplier roster includes NAURA and AMEC in equipment, NSIG / Shanghai Xinsheng in wafers, Empyrean in EDA, and HiSilicon and UNISOC in fabless design. It retains SMIC, Hua Hong, CXMT, YMTC, JCET and Tongfu in their relevant roles. No numerical Chinese allocation or equal-capability claim was invented. The old leading-edge share row was removed rather than implying that a foundry roster measures node-specific output. The DUV immersion examples use Nikon; Canon was removed from that particular subset. Western Digital was omitted from the qualitative NAND roster rather than carrying forward an outdated company-group label.
- `chain-map`: replaced generic Chinese smelters with a named example, Hoshine, alongside Ferroglobe and Elkem. Added NSIG / Shanghai Xinsheng to crystal/wafer examples and China to those production-region lists. Removed the unsupported smelter percentage and the five-firm 300 mm share claim from these example lists. Clarified that downstream stages follow a high-end GPU case, not a complete global supplier census.
- `export-timeline`: replaced viewpoint-dependent Allied control / China response labels with named jurisdictions and equivalent Policy / industry labels. China now uses a neutral palette token instead of the error/danger color. Filters, accessible labels and detail headings follow that convention. Removed motive attribution about a signal to Washington, loophole framing, categorical smuggling impossibility, undifferentiated equipment-vendor revenue-loss claims, and unsupported worse-yield / impossible-node assertions. The Mate 60 entry distinguishes a working chip from evidence about yield, capacity and cost; its untraceable capacity estimate was removed. Event IDs, actor keys, navigation and dated legal-event data remain stable.
- `node-table`: explicitly scopes its numerical comparison to selected Intel, TSMC and Samsung processes. No unsupported SMIC dimensional data was added to make the table appear exhaustive.

## Retained distinctions and limits

The NAND manufacturing widget already includes YMTC as a named vendor; it was not changed merely to add another country label. Physics and process figures remain geographic-neutral where nationality contributes nothing to their teaching purpose. Electronic-grade and solar-grade polysilicon remain distinct; documented production of one grade is not evidence of qualification for another. A specific GPU/CoWoS/GB200 supplier example is not a claim that other packaging or system suppliers lack capability. No hypothetical company was inserted into a particular GPU bill of materials without qualification evidence.

The timeline still contains inherited dated policy descriptions and numerical thresholds. This pass does not certify all those historical assertions or make the timeline a current licensing guide. Individual product qualification, manufacturing yield, capacity and market share require their own evidence. Supplier catalogs establish offered roles, not equivalence between all listed companies or independent confirmation of marketing performance claims.

- [Hoshine company profile](https://www.hoshinesilicon.com/en/gsjs/index_69.aspx.html): industrial silicon business, headquarters and production bases. This does not establish electronic-grade polysilicon qualification.
- [HiSilicon company profile](https://www.hisilicon.com/cn/about-us/company): semiconductor design company. [UNISOC's own June 2025 release](https://www.prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/unisoc-completed-optimized-to-support-android-16-delivering-enhanced-user-experience-302482121.html) identifies its fabless business; this is a company-authored release, not independent performance verification.
- [Nikon lithography lineup](https://www.nikon.com/business/semi/lineup/): named ArF immersion scanners. [Nikon multiple-patterning explanation](https://www.nikonprecision.com/products-and-technology/immersion-and-multiple-patterning/): role of repeated patterning and overlay control.
- [Dutch government, 2024-09-06](https://www.government.nl/latest/news/2024/09/06/the-netherlands-expands-export-control-measure-advanced-semiconductor-manufacturing-equipment): export authorization assessed case by case, distinct from a blanket export ban. Used to correct the explanatory distinction, not to infer current shipment eligibility.
