# Review: Module 20 — Economics, Geography and Geopolitics of the Chain
Reviewer summary: The module's structure, mechanisms and export-control chronology are sound, and almost every policy date (Oct 2022, Oct 2023, Dec 2024, Jan/May/Aug/Sept/Nov 2025, Jan 2026), CHIPS award, and Intel equity figure checked out against primary sources. The problems were concentrated in market-size and company-revenue figures that had drifted or were simply wrong: 2025 WSTS chip sales were $791.7B (not $772B, which was the autumn forecast), SEMI total equipment 2025 was $135B (not $125–130B), ASML's 2024 China share was 41% (not 49%), TSMC shipped ~15M (not ~17M) wafers in 2025, ASE's revenue is ~$20B (not $30B), Apple's TSMC purchases are ~$25–30B (not $60B+), and Broadcom's semiconductor revenue is ~$38B (not $50–60B). Several 2025–26 events were stale as of September 2026 and were refreshed with "as of" dates: hyperscaler 2026 capex guidance (~$720–745B after the August 2026 round), H200 China status (token shipments, production halted March 2026), JASM 2 (construction Oct 2025, upgraded to 3 nm Feb 2026), Intel 14A (PDK 0.5/0.9, not 1.0; Intel committed to 14A in June 2026), and Rapidus public support (~¥2.35T). The worked example on moving 1% of TSMC capacity was re-computed on the corrected wafer count and the quiz updated to match.
Overall verdict: Needed several corrections

## Remaining caveats
- Wafer prices, capex per 1,000 wspm, design costs (IBS), SMIC yields and capacity, CXMT capacity share, Huawei Ascend unit volumes and CoWoS/HBM capacity figures are analyst estimates; the module hedges them with "~" and they should be read as ranges.
- Foundry-market totals depend on whether TrendForce's top-10 or a broader definition is used; the table now uses top-10-based sums (~$170–175B for 2025).
- Hyperscaler capex guidance changes every quarter; the figures are as of the August 2026 earnings round and the next revision (late October 2026) will move them again.
- H200/China status is fluid; the module states the position as of NVIDIA's July 2026 10-Q.
- The ~$1B smuggling figure is a press estimate (FT), not a government number.
- Korea's memory shares are by revenue (DRAM ~70%); bit-share figures differ slightly.
