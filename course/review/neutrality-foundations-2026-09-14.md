## Quiz 01, question 5

Answer index remains **1**. The purpose remains distinguishing solar volume from electronic-grade qualification.

Before question: “Why do the world's 300 mm wafer makers buy electronic-grade polysilicon almost entirely from Wacker, Hemlock, Tokuyama, OCI, and High-Purity Silicon Corp. (the former Mitsubishi Materials business), even though Chinese producers such as Tongwei, GCL, and Daqo make roughly 95% of the world's polysilicon?”

After question: “Why does a large solar-polysilicon producer not automatically become a qualified electronic-grade supplier for every 300 mm wafer process?”

| Option index | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Chinese plants use the FBR silane route, which cannot reach better than 6N purity | FBR chemistry cannot produce semiconductor-purity silicon, regardless of process control |
| 1 (correct) | Electronic grade requires 10-100x lower boron and phosphorus, etched and cleanroom-handled chunk, FZ test-ingot verification and 1-2 year customer qualification, and the whole EG market is only ~40-50 kt/yr, so incumbents with long-term contracts and traceable supply chains keep it | Electronic-grade supply needs stringent impurity and surface-cleanliness control, verification and customer qualification; solar tonnage alone does not demonstrate those requirements |
| 2 | US export controls prohibit the import of any Chinese polysilicon for semiconductor use | Every semiconductor customer requires its polysilicon to be manufactured in the same country as its wafer factory |
| 3 | Chinese polysilicon is sold only as granules, which cannot be loaded into a Czochralski crucible | Granular silicon cannot be loaded into a Czochralski crucible |

The wrong options remain wrong; their national stereotypes were removed. The explanation no longer asserts that Chinese electronic-grade products supply mainly 200 mm/domestic customers. It explains process qualification and names Xinhua as one electronic-grade supplier.

## Primary sources and limits

- [Hoshine silicon-metal catalogue](https://www.hoshinesilicon.com/en/products/list_78.aspx-2.html?lcid=40): offered silicon-metal products, not universal semiconductor-feedstock qualification.
- [GCL semiconductor-materials portfolio](https://www.gcl-power.com/en/goods/semiconductor.html): Xinhua electronic-grade polysilicon. Did not repeat self-reported global rank or equate GCL’s entire solar output with this product.
- [NSIG](https://www.nsig.com/): indexed official page identifies Zing’s commercial 300 mm wafers. Direct full-page retrieval was unreliable; this supports the narrow named business, not a new capacity or yield estimate.
- [JSG](https://www.jsjd.cc/index.html): IC-grade 8–12 inch silicon growth/processing equipment, separately from its photovoltaic equipment.
- [Yunnan Germanium zone-refined ingot catalogue](https://www.sino-ge.com/view/ynzyPC/7/501/view/1226.html): official indexed product entry; full-page retrieval timed out. Limited to the stated ingot role, without new purity, share or customer claims.
- [Anji products](https://www.anjimicro.com/en/jiejuefangan.html): CMP slurries and formulated wet chemicals.
- [NAURA products](https://www.naura.com/product/), [Piotech products](https://en.piotech.cn/), [AMEC 2024 ESG report](https://www.amec-inc.com/uploads/files/20250611/17496080859885.pdf): process categories and product families. Company reports do not independently establish parity, every customer’s acceptance or worldwide installed base.
- [ACM wet processing](https://www.acmr.com/tools-and-processes/wet-processing/) and [corporate profile](https://acmresearch.gcs-web.com/corporate-profile/): product role and US-parent/Shanghai-operations distinction.
- [SMIC process portfolio](https://www.smics.com/en/site/productdetail/2): foundry role; not a new quantified leading-edge share.
- [Kingstone catalogue](https://www.kingstonesemi.com/en/products): high-current, medium-current, high-energy, hydrogen and compound-semiconductor implant families.
- [SMEE IC projection catalogue](https://www.smee.com.cn/eis.pub?differ_net=0&method=indexinfo&onclicknodeno=1_4_1_1&service=homepageService): public 90 nm-class IC equipment category. No assertion of qualified immersion or EUV delivery was inferred.
- [TechInsights Kirin9000S analysis](https://www.techinsights.com/blog/hisilicon-kirin-9000s-smic-7nm-n2-process-flow-analysis): primary physical device analysis, not a fab-wide yield or economics study.
- [SSMB experimental mechanism, Nature2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03203-0), [further SSMB tests, Communications Physics2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42005-024-01657-y): actual experiments; not an industrial13.5nm wafer scanner demonstration.
- [ASML 2025 annual report](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026011378/asml-20251231.htm) and [2024 export-licensing notice](https://www.asml.com/en/news/press-releases/2024/asml-expects-impact-of-updated-export-restrictions-to-fall-within-outlook-for-2025): commercial EUV supplier role and product-specific licensing, not blanket legal clearance for other equipment.
