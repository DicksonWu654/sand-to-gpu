# Review: Module 01 — Sand to Polysilicon
Reviewer summary: This is a strong module. The chemistry (chlorosilane boiling points, reaction enthalpies, segregation coefficients, radiation arithmetic, FTIR lines, SEMI standard numbers) checks out line by line, and the worked examples are arithmetically correct. The fixes were at the margins: the Si-O bond energy quoted (~800 kJ/mol) is the diatomic SiO value rather than the lattice bond (~450 kJ/mol); China's share of silicon metal is ~85% by USGS count, not three quarters; the CO2 parenthetical implied stoichiometry that gives 3.1 t, not 5 t; the non-China polysilicon price is ~$19–22/kg rather than $20–27/kg; and the Chinese crucible maker OJing is in Inner Mongolia, not Jinzhou.
Overall verdict: Accurate with minor fixes

## Edits applied
- Intro: Si-O bond energy corrected to ~450 kJ/mol per bond (with the ~800 kJ/mol diatomic value explained).
- Section 1 worked example: 1 ppma boron resistivity widened to ~0.3–0.5 Ω·cm.
- Section 3.3 worked example: MG-Si price reconciled to ~$1,500–2,500/t (consistent with Section 9.1); CO2 footprint changed to ~4–5 t/t with the stoichiometric 3.1 t and real ~1.2 t fixed-carbon consumption explained.
- Section 3.4: China share corrected to ~80–85% with USGS 2024 figures (4.6 Mt world, 3.9 Mt China) and a note that Chinese industry data run higher.
- Section 5.3: REC Silicon shutdown timing (announced Dec 2024, ceased early 2025) and failure reason clarified; Wacker FBR sized (~650 t/yr, since 2009).
- Section 9.1: non-China polysilicon price corrected to ~$19–22/kg (OPIS GPM).
- Section 10.1: "Ojing (Jinzhou) and Ningbo" replaced with OJing Technology (Inner Mongolia) plus Jinzhou/Jiangsu cluster; crucible price hedged to "several thousand dollars" to match Module 02's range.
- Key Numbers: silicon-metal row (4.6 Mt, ~85%) and polysilicon price row (~$19–22/kg) updated to match body.
- Quiz: no changes; all six answers verified against the module.

## Remaining caveats
- Electronic-grade polysilicon price (~$20–40/kg) and EG demand (~40–50 kt/yr) are estimates; contracts are private.
- Semiconductor crucible price and graphite hot-zone costs are order-of-magnitude figures.
- GCL's "about a quarter of Chinese output in H1 2025" is consistent with its 480 kt capacity but not independently confirmed.
- Chinese polysilicon prices moved within RMB 35–60/kg in 2025–26; the ~$5–8/kg range covers that swing but any single month may sit outside it.
- Ferroglobe's ~350 kt/yr silicon-metal figure is nameplate-ish; actual output has been lower in 2024–25.
